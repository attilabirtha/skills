# proClick slide compositions

Use these as compositional guidance. Coordinates below are new defaults for a 1280 × 720 canvas, not a rigid template. Keep user-specified dimensions and supplied layouts when applicable.

## Cover

Use a short subject title and one clarifying line. Official logo at a natural anchor. A full-width team photo can work if all team members remain visible and text stays clear of faces. Otherwise use a contained photo beside the text. Select white or standard logo for sufficient contrast. Do not automatically add partner badges, dates, pricing, or agency statistics.

## Editorial / explanation

Paper background, ink heading, 64–72 px outer margins. Title around y=90–180. One explanation and one supporting figure, or two balanced text columns. Optional coral topic label only when it adds useful navigation. Prefer enough space to scan the content without boxes around each sentence.

## Dark strategy or section break

Charcoal surface, white heading, pale supporting text. Coral emphasizes the decision or key stage. Keep the amount of content lower than on a normal content slide. Pale-green recommendation treatment belongs on light backgrounds unless contrast has been checked.

## Process

Use a linear sequence of two to five meaningful stages, each with a short title and concise description. Preserve causal order. Use native editable connectors when needed, not decorative arrows embedded in an image. If labels wrap excessively, split the process across slides rather than shrinking the copy.

## Product screenshot

Two useful formats:

- Full screen: short title at y=40–110; screenshot approximately x=64, y=135, w=1152, h=500, contained with original aspect ratio; concise caption below.
- Detail: screenshot or clearly identified crop occupying about two-thirds of the canvas, with one explanatory column. Keep the image's original content and the slide's annotations distinct.

Place “Illustrative mockup” outside the screenshot as editable text when the embedded disclosure is too small at slide scale. Mention proposed functionality or fictional metrics where material. Do not regenerate the entire slide as one raster image.

## Evidence / results

One editable chart or table with a conclusion the data actually supports. Title, units, reporting period, baseline, and source should be clear. Coral can highlight the selected series, with muted secondary series. Do not fabricate result numbers merely to fill a metric layout. Keep demo data outside real-results sections.

## Commercial scope

Use an editable table when packages or cost components need comparison. Keep setup, recurring service, usage and media costs separate if they apply. Show tax basis and currency. Include only categories present in the brief. Use the price-offer skill for a standalone offer PDF, not as the slide-writing engine.

## Decision / close

State the decision, the inputs needed, and a concrete next action. A short contact line is enough. Use the current brief to decide whether an appendix belongs before or after the close.

## Review

Render all slides and inspect at readable scale. Verify official logo fidelity, selected palette, fonts, photo framing, chart arithmetic, screenshot disclosures, editable evidence, and source notes. Successful package validation does not establish visual quality or verify claims.
