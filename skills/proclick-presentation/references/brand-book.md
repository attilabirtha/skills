# proClick Brand Book

Version 1.0 · 10 September 2026

This foundation consolidates the current website, the existing agency presentation, and the proclick-price-offer skill. It documents observed identity and introduces explicitly marked production defaults. It is a working brand standard, not a claim that a formal corporate manual previously existed.

## 1. Brand foundation

**Name:** proClick. Preserve this capitalization in prose; use official artwork for the wordmark. URLs and email addresses remain lowercase.

**Positioning:** an ecommerce performance marketing agency combining human expertise with AI-supported processes. The public site emphasizes profitable growth, measurement, and execution. This is the foundation for communication, not a guarantee of financial results.

**Audience:** ecommerce founders, marketing leaders, and operations teams who need clear commercial choices and accountable execution.

**Voice:** direct, competent, commercially specific, and human. Explain the work and its relevance before listing tools. Distinguish a measured result from a target, an example, or a proposed capability.

Website phrase retained as a short reference: “Expertiză umană, procese AI native.” Source: [proclick.ro](https://proclick.ro/), accessed 10 September 2026. Translations and new taglines are editorial adaptations, not additional official slogans.

### Writing examples (new editorial guidance)

| Avoid | Prefer |
| --- | --- |
| We transform ecommerce with revolutionary AI | We connect order data to support so the team can handle delivery cases with less manual lookup |
| Guaranteed growth | We agree the baseline and evaluate results against the target |
| All-in-one solutions for everything | Name the workflow, the owner, and the next action |

Use the language requested by the audience. Romanian, English, and Hungarian are supported editorial contexts; preserve diacritics and verify glyph rendering. Do not use a fixed Romanian-only rule for all presentations.

## 2. Logo system

Use `../assets/proclick_logo.png` on white or warm paper. Use `../assets/proclick_logo_white.png` on charcoal or a sufficiently dark photographic surface. Both are supplied artwork; preserve the original proportions and colors.

Do not redraw the logo, change the case of letters inside the artwork, stretch it, place it on a visually competing surface, or add shadows and decorative outlines. AI-generated logos in mockups are illustrative and do not replace the master assets.

New production defaults: keep at least half the displayed logo height as clear space. On a 1280 × 720 slide, use approximately 140–162 px width for routine branding. Increase it on a cover only when the composition needs it. These are layout defaults, not historic trademark specifications.

### Use and contrast

Use ink for most text, paper or white for most surfaces, and coral for focal emphasis. Use dark sections to mark a change in the story. Preserve original colors inside screenshots, partner marks, and source charts when they carry meaning.

New accessibility guidance: check the rendered text/background combination. Use a 4.5:1 contrast target for normal text and 3:1 for large text. Bright coral and white do not automatically satisfy normal-text contrast. Prefer ink body text, white on charcoal, and coral for large labels or non-text accents. Use labels or patterns as well as color in charts. This is production guidance, not a certification claim.

## 4. Typography and spacing

Observed website font: system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, sans-serif. The agency slide layouts record Aptos Display for headings and Aptos for body text. The price-offer generator uses Arial and Arial Bold.

For new presentations, prefer the agency font pair when available. Use Arial/Arial Bold as an explicit fallback. Keep font licenses with any externally supplied font files; no proprietary fonts are bundled here.

New 16:9 defaults, on a 1280 × 720 canvas: cover title 56–72 px; slide heading 42–50 px; body 24–30 px; secondary text 20–24 px; source notes 16–18 px. These are CSS pixel units (96 DPI), not PowerPoint points. Multiply by 0.75 to convert to points. Cut or split content before shrinking it below a readable size. Match a user-supplied template when it specifies a different scale.

Use a 64–72 px left/right content margin and a consistent title baseline. Allow generous separation between groups. Keep footer content minimal. A small coral rule, discreet logo, and page number are enough when the layout needs them.

## 5. Photography and product images

Use genuine team photography from the supplied assets. The wide team photo is appropriate for a landscape cover; the portrait full-team composition suits a side panel or portrait document. Team membership may change, so verify that a chosen photo fits the brief before describing it as the current full team.

Preserve image aspect ratio. Do not crop team members out to force a full-bleed cover or cover faces with headlines. Dark overlays are an available treatment, not a requirement. A contained image beside a headline is equally valid.

Avoid generic robots, stock handshakes, invented client logos, and images presented as evidence of real results. Use relevant product screenshots when the subject is a product or workflow.

### Screenshot handling

Keep the reference interface's structure, relative spacing, and icon conventions when creating related mockups. Use the official logo asset when compositing deterministically. Treat image-generated wordmarks as approximations. Label generated screens “Illustrative mockup” and sample metrics “Fictional data”. Do not present generated UI as proof of a shipped feature.

For slides, show one screen large enough to read. Use a separate detail slide if the full interface is too dense. Keep captions and callouts editable. Label a crop or highlight when it changes the visible scope of the screenshot.

## 6. Presentation grammar

The agency reference uses warm cream pages, charcoal strategy pages, coral markers, white modules, thin rules, and occasional pale-green conclusions. Adapt that vocabulary to the content instead of reproducing every card and label.

Use a full-width composition where possible. Native tables and charts should remain editable. Use a service grid when comparing actual services; use a process layout for sequential work; use a full-width screenshot for a product walkthrough. Do not make every slide a dashboard.

Suggested narrative: audience problem, relevant approach, specific workflow, scope, evidence, rollout, decision. This is optional and does not impose a fixed slide count. Omit sections that do not support the user's purpose.

Keep evidence, assumptions, examples, and proposed capabilities distinct. Footnote time-sensitive facts in slide notes. Recheck partner status and agency numbers before reuse. A client logo in a historic deck is not authorization to invent an endorsement.

## 7. Commercial documents

Standalone commercial offer PDFs remain the responsibility of `proclick-price-offer`. Its offer palette and service modules remain valid when that skill is used. This book does not rewrite the existing skill or website.

Use one currency and one clearly stated tax basis per commercial comparison. Separate implementation, recurring services, media spend, and metered platform costs when relevant. Use customer-approved amounts. Do not turn historic examples into current prices, assume a tax rate, or insert PPC budget allocations into unrelated product presentations.
