# Commercial service-cover master

Use this layout for a new proClick commercial service briefing when the user asks for the established Email Marketing / price-offer presentation look, or does not supply a competing cover design. It is a reusable visual master for service decks, not a requirement for case studies, reports, or user-supplied templates.

## Composition

Use the official wide team photo across the whole 16:9 slide. Keep every person visible. Place a charcoal overlay above the photo at approximately 55–60% opacity; the people should remain recognizable, but the copy must read first.

On a 1280 × 720 slide, use about 64 px outer margins:

- upper right: white proClick logo, about 150 px wide;
- upper left: a short coral rule, then a compact uppercase offer or audit kicker;
- left content area: editable white title, maximum two lines, 52–58 px; then one explanatory line at about 20–22 px;
- lower third: three equal, flush rectangular modules, alternating paper / charcoal / paper. Each has a coral or white heading and a single short line of supporting text;
- beneath the band: coral `OBIECTIV` label and one clear white objective sentence; use a quiet footer with service name at left and page number at right.

The kicker and objective must use facts from the brief. When an audit is free only for existing proClick clients, say that eligibility in the kicker and repeat it on the activation slide.

## Content mapping

Use the three modules to summarize three genuinely distinct service dimensions, for example:

| Dimension | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Support | Comenzi și post-cumpărare |
| Sales | Follow-up și reactivare |
| Canale | WhatsApp, telefon, e-mail |

Adapt the labels and details to the service. Do not force a third module when the brief has fewer than three meaningful dimensions.

## Rendering and quality gate

Render the cover before delivery and check it beside the Email Marketing master when that deck is available. A cover passes only when:

- the background image sits behind every shape and text object;
- the overlay produces the same dark, high-contrast reading surface as the master;
- the title, subtitle, band, and footer do not overlap and remain readable at normal slide size;
- the team remains visible in the whole image, and no face is unintentionally cropped;
- band modules have equal widths, aligned baselines, and concise copy.

If the authoring engine places an image above text or ignores overlay opacity, repair the stacking order or embed the photo as a proper background image, then render the cover again. Do not accept a structurally valid deck whose cover fails this visual check.
