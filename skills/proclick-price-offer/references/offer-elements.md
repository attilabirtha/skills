# ProClick Offer Elements

Use this reference when an offer needs more detail than the default five-page structure.

## Pricing

- Choose one pricing mode for the whole document:
  - `TVA inclus` as primary values, with net values below in smaller muted text; or
  - `net + TVA` as primary values when the user asks for prices without VAT.
- Keep all totals consistent across cover, budget narrative, channel/media page, and commercial page.
- If the user asks for EUR, keep service fees and media budgets in EUR across all affected pages.
- Use explicit `+ TVA` wording when prices are shown net and tax still applies.
- Do not mix `TVA inclus` and `net + TVA` styles in the same offer unless the user explicitly asks for both.

## Service Modules

Use six service cards by default. Expand wording only when the user asks for more detail.

- Strategie de lansare: positioning, funnel, segmentation, 90-day calendar, message directions, product prioritization.
- Audit tracking: GA4, GTM, Meta Pixel, Shopify events, Google Ads conversions, UTM structure, data quality.
- Administrare PPC: Google Search/Shopping/PMax, Meta prospecting and remarketing, catalog ads, creative testing, sales optimization.
- Optimizare conversii: UX audit for homepage, collections, product pages, cart, checkout, and CRO priorities.
- Optimizare feed + CSS: product titles, categories, attributes, Shopping feed, CSS Partner benefit up to 20% discount.
- Raportare consolidata: dashboard and monthly reporting for spend, revenue, ROAS, POAS, CAC, AOV, winning products.

## Strategy Blocks

Three-stage launch page:

- Pregatire: tracking, Shopify, feed, KPI, product priorities, first creative directions.
- Lansare: Google for demand capture; Meta for demand generation, social proof, catalog ads, remarketing.
- Optimizare: move budget toward channels, audiences, creatives, and products that are sellable for promotion and sales.

Use this line when expectations need calibration:

`Pentru [CLIENT], prima luna trebuie sa valideze trackingul, mesajele si produsele vandabile pentru promovare si vanzare. Abia dupa aceea crestem presiunea media.`

## Channels And Media Allocation

Pick the mix based on the offer objective, total media budget, available creative assets, and how broad the launch needs to be.

Focused eCommerce launch mix when concentration matters more than channel coverage:

- Google Ads: 40%
- Meta Ads: 60%

Complete eCommerce launch mix when the offer should cover all major paid channels:

- Google Ads: 30%
- Meta Ads: 45%
- TikTok Ads: 10%
- YouTube Ads: 10%
- Pinterest Ads: 5%

Channel roles:

- Google Ads: for search demand capture, Shopping/PMax, CSS Partner advantage, and high-intent traffic.
- Meta Ads: for visual prospecting, catalog ads, remarketing, social proof, add-to-cart and checkout optimization.
- TikTok Ads: for short-form video hook tests and new audiences.
- YouTube Ads: for video reach, remarketing, and search lift.
- Pinterest Ads: for decor, lifestyle, gifting, interior, and inspiration-led categories.

Do not include every channel automatically when the media budget is too small, creative assets are missing, or the user asks to keep services and budgets focused. If the user asks for a complete mix, include all relevant channels and make the percentages sum to 100%.

If the user excludes channels from the scope, remove them from:

- service descriptions;
- channel tables;
- commercial wording;
- narrative recommendations.

Example: if `Pinterest` and `LinkedIn` are out, the offer should refer only to `Meta` and `TikTok` where relevant.

For social-only offers, a channel-role table is often clearer than a percentage-allocation table.

## PPC KPI Set

Keep KPIs PPC-specific unless the user asks for broader business metrics:

- Vizibilitate: spend, reach, impressions, CPM, frequency.
- Trafic: CTR, CPC, clicks, landing page views, traffic quality.
- Conversii: add-to-cart, checkout initiated, purchase, cost per purchase, conversion rate.
- Eficienta: ROAS, conversion value, cost per acquisition, budget moved to winning campaigns.

## Reporting

Default reporting cadence:

- Weekly short report: budget evolution, conclusions, actions performed.
- Monthly extended report: results, content recommendations, Shopify/CRO recommendations, next-month plan.
- Biweekly alignment meeting: commercial decisions, channel priorities, creative test validation.

## Cover Layout

- Prefer a wide team photo when the cover should show the full team.
- Preserve the source image aspect ratio; never stretch the image to fill the page.
- When the image includes visible faces, move the headline/subheadline lower if needed so faces remain unobstructed.
- It is acceptable to leave extra space above or below the image if that avoids distortion.

## Commercial Notes

- A commercial page can use `Conditii de colaborare` or another short label instead of `Ipoteze de lucru` when the tone needs to feel firmer and cleaner.
- Keep scope notes short and operational: what is included, what is treated separately, and what the fees cover.

## Validation Checklist

Before delivering:

- Render each affected PDF page to PNG.
- Check no text is clipped.
- Check logo contrast on every page.
- Check service fee, media budget, and total budget reconcile.
- Check channels in services, strategy, and channel/media table match each other.
- Check the chosen pricing mode is used consistently.
- Check cover photography is not distorted and important faces are not blocked by text.
