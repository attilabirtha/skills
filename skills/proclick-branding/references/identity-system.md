# proClick Identity System

This reference consolidates the shared, reusable identity decisions from the proClick presentation and price-offer workflows. It is production guidance, not a replacement for a client-supplied template or formal trademark specification.

## Precedence and scope

Use this order when sources differ:

1. Explicit user direction and approved supplied materials.
2. The relevant artifact-specific skill or template.
3. This identity system and its selected visual mode.

Do not combine values from different modes merely because their colors are similar. Keep the selected mode coherent within an artifact.

## Name and voice

- Write the brand as **proClick**; use the supplied wordmark artwork rather than typesetting, redrawing, or generating a logo.
- The voice is direct, competent, commercially specific, and human. Explain the work and its relevance before listing tools.
- State whether a number or outcome is evidence, a target, an example, or a proposed capability. Do not promise results.
- Romanian, English, and Hungarian are supported contexts. Preserve diacritics and verify the chosen font supports the language.

Use ink for body text, paper/white for ordinary surfaces, coral for large labels or a focused emphasis, and white on dark surfaces. Aim for 4.5:1 contrast for normal text and 3:1 for large text. Coral and white are not automatically a valid normal-text pairing.

## Typography and spacing

- Presentation: Aptos Display headings and Aptos body when available; Arial/Arial Bold is the practical fallback.
- Offer: Arial and Arial Bold.
- Web: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`.

For new 16:9 work, these are useful production defaults: 56–72 px cover title, 42–50 px heading, 24–30 px body, 20–24 px secondary copy, 16–18 px notes, and 64–72 px outer margins. Cut or split content before reducing it below readable size. These are new production defaults, not historic corporate specifications.

## Logo and imagery

- Use the standard logo on white or warm-paper surfaces and the white logo on charcoal or a sufficiently dark photo.
- Keep at least half the displayed logo height as clear space. On a 1280 × 720 layout, routine logo width is approximately 140–162 px.
- Preserve original proportions and colors; avoid shadows, outlines, and competing backgrounds.
- Use genuine supplied team photography. The wide image suits landscape covers; the portrait composition suits portrait work or a side panel.
- Keep people inside the frame and type away from faces. A contained photo alongside text is preferable to a distorted full-bleed crop.

## Content and commercial boundaries

- Do not present generic stock imagery, generated logos, fictional product screens, historic client logos, or invented results as real evidence. Label illustrative mockups and fictional data clearly.
- Use editable charts, tables, labels, and annotations where the artifact permits it.
- In commercial contexts, keep one-time setup/audit/project fees, first-month fees, recurring services, platform usage, and media spend distinct by period and payer. State a currency and tax basis only when approved information is available.
- Use the formal offer workflow for payment schedules, conditional audit credits, fee waivers, and legal/commercial terms. Do not imply that a branded web page, post, or deck replaces those documents.

## Final check

- Correct logo asset, contrast, clear space, and aspect ratio.
- One palette mode, with no accidental cross-mode token mixing.
- Readable type, appropriate language glyphs, and unobscured people in photographs.
- Claims, partner status, metrics, rates, endorsements, and legal details are current and sourced.
- Illustrative material is labeled and no template/example figure is framed as a client outcome.
